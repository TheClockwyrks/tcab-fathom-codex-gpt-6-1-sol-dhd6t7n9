# Fathom

Carry a little light into a very deep dark. Fathom drops you into a flooded maze with a belly full of hunger and just enough glow to find the next bend. Graze the plankton and your circle of vision swells, bringing the trench's cold stone and drifting lights into view.

Light is life. It is also a liability. The Lanternjaw follows your glow, hiding behind an amber bulb that looks just like a harmless bonus drifter. The eyeless Gloamfin listens around corners. The Flarefish burns a sudden window through the rock, then hunts whatever that flash finds.

Send a sonar pulse winding through the passages to remember the way ahead. Watch violet wavefronts approach from the dark, and use ink to break a light hunter's fix before slipping around a bend. Your discoveries stay remembered, but this Kindle dive hides even familiar ground beyond the circle you carry.

Eat the last mote to clear the maze and descend. Your score and remaining lives follow you down; more hunters and a shorter sonar reach make each new depth a tighter negotiation between hunger, silence, and the next little light.
